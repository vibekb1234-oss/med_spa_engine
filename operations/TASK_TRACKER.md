# Task Tracker

Use this as the master operating board. Keep one active owner per task and one clear next action.

## Sales Board

| Task | Status | Owner | Due | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Build weekly lead list | Backlog | Owner | Weekly Monday | 50-100 qualified leads. |
| Send first-touch outbound | Backlog | Owner | Weekly Tuesday | Email, DM, or website form. |
| Follow up open conversations | Backlog | Owner | Daily | 24h, 72h, 7d cadence. |
| Book demos | Backlog | Owner | Daily | Every engaged lead gets a binary next step. |
| Send proposals | Backlog | Owner | Same day as call | Include one recommended package. |

## Retention Board

## Status Rules

Use only:

- Backlog
- Next
- In progress
- Waiting on client
- Waiting on tool
- QA
- Done
- Parked

## Priority Rules

- P0: blocks paid launch or client-facing function.
- P1: blocks delivery timeline.
- P2: improves client value or reduces support.
- P3: polish or future improvement.
