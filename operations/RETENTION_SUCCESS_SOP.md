# Retention And Customer Success SOP

## Retention Goal

Keep clients because the system is visibly working, not because they forgot to cancel.

Medspa owners churn when they stop feeling the value. Retention depends on visible recovered-revenue moments every week and a simple monthly story that connects the system to missed consult recovery, no-show recovery, lapsed VIP reactivation, reviews, referrals, and owner visibility.

## Monthly Success Cadence

| Timing | Action |
|---|---|
| Weekly | Confirm weekly performance report sent. |
| Weekly | Check Workflow 7 error log. |
| Monthly | Send owner summary with wins, leaks, and next actions. |
| Monthly | Review no-show recovery, review ask, VIP retention, and lapsed-client performance. |
| Quarterly | Propose one improvement or campaign. |

## Monthly Report Template

Subject: Monthly MGE summary for {{Clinic Name}}

Sections:

- Completed treatments
- Revenue tracked
- No-shows and recovery actions
- New inquiries
- Review/referral actions
- Lapsed clients surfaced
- Workflow errors, if any
- Recommended next improvement

## Weekly Win Capture

Every Friday, log 3 small wins before the owner asks for proof:

- One missed consult or cold lead that got follow-up.
- One no-show, late-cancel, or reminder action.
- One lapsed VIP, review request, referral ask, or repeat-booking action.

Use names only when appropriate for the owner report. The point is to make the system feel alive and valuable.

## Support Boundaries

Included:

- Workflow uptime checks
- Minor copy edits
- Fixes caused by workflow/dashboard issues
- Monthly reporting
- Basic troubleshooting

Billable change work:

- New CRM/booking platform integration
- New workflow outside the 8-workflow package
- Custom analytics
- Multi-location setup
- Major copy/campaign rebuild
- Staff training beyond agreed handoff

## Churn Prevention Signals

Act fast if:

- Owner stops opening reports.
- Workflow errors repeat twice.
- Clinic says "we are not sure it is doing anything."
- Staff bypasses the system.
- Sheet has stale or messy data.
- No dashboard login in 14 days.

## Save Plays

- Show before/after activity log.
- Pull 3 examples of follow-up that would have been missed.
- Run a 30-day no-show/rebooking review.
- Offer a focused campaign refresh.
- Tighten dashboard settings and labels.

## Upsell Triggers

- More than one location.
- Heavy paid ad spend.
- Owner wants SMS.
- Owner wants seasonal campaigns.
- Staff wants more automations.
- Clinic asks for monthly promotional calendar.
