# AI Communication System

This file defines how AI helps communicate with leads and clients without losing trust, sounding robotic, or creating compliance risk.

## Communication Principle

AI prepares the work. The owner controls the trust.

Use AI for speed, structure, and consistency. Use human judgment for price, trust, complaints, sensitive access, client conflict, and final sales moments.

## Communication Channels

| Channel | Use | AI role | Owner role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold email | First-touch and follow-ups | Draft, personalize, queue | Approve campaign and send strategy |
| Instagram DM | Short local outreach | Draft and classify replies | Send manually where needed |
| LinkedIn DM | Owner/manager route | Draft and track | Personalize important contacts |
| Website form | Manual lead contact | Draft short form note | Submit manually |
| Sales follow-up | After discovery/demo | Draft recap and objection response | Send or edit |
| Client email | Onboarding and weekly reports | Draft updates and summaries | Approve sensitive updates |
| Support messages | Client questions | Draft response | Handle anything risky or emotional |

## Reply Classification

| Classification | Meaning | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| Hot | Wants details, pricing, or call | Owner responds and books call |
| Warm | Interested but not urgent | Send helpful answer and ask qualifying question |
| Objection | Concern about price, time, need, tech, or trust | Draft objection response for owner approval |
| Not now | Timing issue | Add nurture date |
| Referral | Sent to another person | Contact referred person and thank original |
| Unsubscribe | Does not want contact | Stop all outreach |
| Bad fit | Not a medspa, no need, or wrong buyer | Disqualify |
| Complaint | Upset or sensitive response | Owner handles only |

## Tone Rules

Use:

- Short sentences.
- Specific clinic observations.
- Plain language.
- One clear next step.
- Conservative claims.

Avoid:

- "Revolutionary AI".
- "Guaranteed revenue".
- "We automate everything".
- Fake familiarity.
- Long paragraphs.
- Pressure-heavy sales language.

## Escalation Rules

Escalate to the owner when:

- A lead asks for pricing.
- A lead asks for contract terms.
- A prospect objects aggressively.
- A client is unhappy.
- A client shares sensitive patient information.
- A client asks for a medical or compliance opinion.
- A workflow failure affects live client communications.
- A payment, refund, or cancellation question appears.

## Response SLA

| Message type | Target |
|---|---|
| Hot lead | Same day |
| Warm lead | Within 24 hours |
| Objection | Within 24 hours |
| Unsubscribe | Same day stop |
| Client blocker | Same day |
| Normal client update | Weekly |

## Quality Check Before Sending

Before any AI-drafted message is sent, check:

- Is the clinic name correct?
- Is the pain specific?
- Is the message short?
- Is there one clear next step?
- Are claims safe?
- Are no secrets or private links included?
- Is the contact allowed to receive this message?

## Generic Outreach Blocker

Block the message if it could be sent to any medspa without changing a word.

Required before approval:

- One real clinic observation.
- One clear suspected leak.
- One sentence connecting the leak to the Lost Revenue Recovery OS.
- One low-friction CTA.

Use this test:

> If the clinic owner cannot tell you actually looked at their business, the message is not ready.
