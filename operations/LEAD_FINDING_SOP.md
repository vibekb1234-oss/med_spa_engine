# Lead Finding SOP

## Weekly Target

Build 50-100 qualified medspa leads per week. Quality matters more than raw volume.

## Source Channels

| Channel | How to use it | Qualification signal |
|---|---|---|
| Google Maps | Search "medspa", "aesthetic clinic", "botox clinic", "laser hair removal" by city | Reviews, website, service list, owner visibility |
| Instagram | Search local medspa hashtags and city tags | Active posting, DMs open, weak booking follow-up |
| Facebook/Meta ads library | Find clinics running offers | They spend on acquisition and may leak follow-up |
| Local directories | Spa/medical aesthetic directories | Contact info and niche fit |
| LinkedIn | Search owners, clinic managers, injectors | Decision maker available |

## Lead Record Fields

Use these columns in the tracker:

- Clinic name
- City/state
- Website
- Instagram
- Owner/manager name
- Email
- Phone
- Services
- Review count
- Rating
- Booking system observed
- Likely pain
- Lead source
- Outreach status
- Last touch date
- Next follow-up date
- Notes

## Qualification Pass

Score each lead 0-2:

- Niche fit: medspa/aesthetic clinic, not generic salon.
- Activity: recent posts, reviews, ads, or active promotions.
- Follow-up pain: forms, DMs, booking links, reviews, or no-show-heavy services.
- Owner access: email, LinkedIn, Instagram, or website form.
- Buying ability: premium services, strong local positioning, visible revenue activity.

Priority leads score 7+ out of 10.

## Daily Lead Block

1. Pick one city or region.
2. Pull 20-30 clinics.
3. Remove bad fits.
4. Enrich owner/contact info.
5. Add likely pain note.
6. Send first touch the same day.

## First-Touch Angles

- "Are inquiries getting followed up within 5 minutes?"
- "Do no-shows trigger a recovery sequence?"
- "Are lapsed Botox/filler clients being reactivated automatically?"
- "Are review asks and referrals happening after every completed visit?"
- "Do you know which clients are at risk before they disappear?"
