# GTM Funnel OS

## Flagship Mechanism

The market positioning should lead with one dominant mechanism:

**Lost Revenue Recovery System**

The system recovers revenue from four clinic leakage points:

- Missed consults and slow inquiry follow-up.
- No-shows and late-canceled appointments.
- Cold leads that never booked.
- Lapsed VIP and repeat-treatment clients.

Reviews, referrals, reminders, dashboard visibility, and reporting are supporting proof. Do not lead sales conversations with a long list of automation features. Lead with recovered revenue, then show which workflows create it.

| Live client | System installed and tested | Handoff + first weekly report | Retainer active |
| Retained | Monthly value proven | Monthly report, support, improvement backlog | Renewal/upsell/churn decision |

## Primary Offer

MedSpa Growth Engine installs a Lost Revenue Recovery System around the clinic's existing booking process: 10 client workflows plus a dashboard that helps recover missed consults, no-shows, cold leads, lapsed clients, reviews, referrals, owner visibility, and weekly opportunity prioritization.

## Qualification Rules

Good fit:

- Independent aesthetic clinic or medspa.
- Owner/operator is reachable.
- Has at least 20 appointments or inquiries per month.
- Uses Gmail/Google Workspace or can set it up.
- Losing money through slow follow-up, no-shows, weak rebooking, missing reviews, or lapsed clients.
- Wants a managed system, not another generic SaaS dashboard.

Bad fit:

- Enterprise chain needing custom integrations and procurement.
- Clinic wants clinical record handling or medical data storage.
- No owner buy-in.
- No willingness to use Google Sheets/n8n style infrastructure.
- Wants guaranteed revenue.

## Conversion Rules

- Sell the lost-revenue moment, show the dashboard, then explain the workflows.
- Keep the close simple: setup fee plus monthly maintenance.
- Do not pitch "AI" as the main value. Pitch recovered missed consults, no-show recovery, lapsed-client reactivation, and cleaner owner visibility.
- Every call ends with a binary next step: paid setup, second decision call, or closed-lost.

## Commoditization Defense

Avoid sounding like a generic GHL, chatbot, or automation agency.

Say:

- "We recover revenue already leaking from your clinic."
- "This is built around medspa follow-up moments: consults, no-shows, reviews, referrals, and VIP cycles."
- "You keep your booking system. We install the recovery layer around it."

Do not say:

- "We build AI automations."
- "We can automate anything."
- "We are a CRM agency."
- "This replaces your booking system."
