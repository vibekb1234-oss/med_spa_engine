# AI Ads And Funnel System

Paid ads should not be the first dependency. Use outbound and manual lead research to learn which hooks get replies first. Once a hook gets real interest, the AI can turn it into ad tests.

## Funnel Offer

Primary front-end offer:

**Free MedSpa Revenue Leak Snapshot**

Simple promise:

> We map where your clinic may be losing booked revenue from missed consults, no-shows, cold leads, and lapsed VIP clients.

Safe claim:

> This is a modeled estimate, not a revenue guarantee.

## Funnel Path

| Step | Asset | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Ad | Meta, Instagram, or local retargeting | Get owner attention |
| Landing page | `landing/index.html` | Explain lost revenue recovery |
| Booking page | `booking/index.html` | Capture clinic leak data |
| AI brief | Internal owner brief | Summarize the prospect before the call |
| Sales call | Owner-led | Confirm pain and close next step |
| Follow-up | Email/DM | Move yes/no decision |

## First Ad Angles

### Angle 1: Missed Consults

Hook:

> Your clinic may not need more leads. It may need faster follow-up on the leads already coming in.

CTA:

> Get the leak snapshot.

### Angle 2: No-Shows

Hook:

> One no-show is annoying. A pattern of no-shows is a revenue leak.

CTA:

> Map the recovery gaps.

### Angle 3: Lapsed VIP Clients

Hook:

> The best revenue in a medspa is usually not the first appointment. It is the repeat visit that never gets chased.

CTA:

> Check the VIP leak.

### Angle 4: Owner Visibility

Hook:

> If you cannot see missed inquiries, no-shows, reviews, and lapsed clients in one place, the leak is harder to fix.

CTA:

> See the recovery system.

## Creative Formats

Use these first:

- Short founder-style video.
- Screen recording of dashboard proof sections.
- Static graphic with "4 leaks we check".
- Simple carousel showing consults, no-shows, cold leads, VIP reactivation.

Avoid:

- Overproduced generic medspa stock ads.
- Fake revenue screenshots.
- Claims like "guaranteed bookings".
- Medical outcome language.

## AI Responsibilities

AI can:

- Draft hooks and scripts.
- Turn winning outbound copy into ad variants.
- Create landing-page test copy.
- Review ad comments and inbox replies.
- Build daily ad performance summaries.
- Suggest pause, keep, or iterate decisions.

Owner must approve:

- Spend.
- Targeting.
- Final creative.
- Claims.
- Landing page offer.
- Any retargeting messages.

## Launch Threshold

Only turn on paid ads when these are true:

- Landing page and booking page are live.
- Booking form routes reliably.
- No fake claims or guarantees are present.
- At least 2 to 3 outbound hooks have shown replies or call interest.
- Owner can take calls within 24 to 48 hours.
- Lead tracker and follow-up queue are ready.

Paid traffic without follow-up discipline will leak money.
