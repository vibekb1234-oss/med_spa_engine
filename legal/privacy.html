Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 - Operator: Bibek Bhandari
Plain-language version: If you ask for a demo, I keep your contact details and your clinic name. If you become a client, the system stores client-facing operational data inside your Google Sheet, in your Google Workspace. I do not host a multi-tenant database. I do not sell data. Medical and clinical records stay in your existing tools - they never enter MedSpa Growth Engine.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how Bibek Bhandari (the operator behind MedSpa Growth Engine, referred to as "I", "me", or "MGE") handles personal data of:

  • Prospects who visit the website, fill out the demo form, or correspond by email.
  • Clinic clients (and their staff) who have purchased the system.
  • Clinic end-clients - i.e. patients/customers of MGE-operated clinics whose data flows through workflows installed for that clinic.

2. What I collect

From prospects

  • Name, work email, phone (if provided), clinic name, city, primary services, weekly appointment volume range, and a free-text description of their biggest follow-up gap.
  • Standard request metadata: IP address, browser/device info, timestamps, referrer.

From clinic clients (during install)

  • Billing details (handled by the payment processor - I do not store full card numbers).
  • Clinic name, owner email, IANA timezone, services offered, Google Maps review URL.
  • OAuth credential IDs for the clinic's Google Workspace - the credentials themselves live inside the clinic's n8n instance, not with me.

From clinic end-clients (the clinic's own clients)

  • Name, email, phone, lead source, service interest, appointment date and status, visit count, lifetime spend, review/referral flags, and opt-out status.
  • This data is stored inside the clinic's own Google Sheet, under the clinic's own Google Workspace. The clinic is the data controller; I act as a service provider to the clinic.

What I never collect

  • Medical records, prescriptions, clinical photos, diagnoses, lab results, or treatment notes.
  • Government identifiers (SSN, driver's licence, passport).
  • Full payment card details (processed externally).
  • Health information beyond service-of-interest tagging.

3. How I use the data

  • Prospect data: to reply to your demo request, schedule a call, and follow up.
  • Clinic data: to install, monitor, and maintain the system; to send the monthly performance email; to assist if a workflow errors.
  • End-client data: only to deliver the workflows the clinic has installed - reminders, review asks, referral asks, retention messages, and the weekly report sent to the clinic owner.

4. Legal basis (for clinics serving EU/UK clients)

For EU/UK residents, processing is based on (a) legitimate interest in operating the service, (b) the clinic's contractual relationship with its end-clients, and (c) consent collected at the point of inquiry. End-clients can withdraw consent at any time via the opt-out link in every automated email.

5. Sharing and sub-processors

The system runs on the clinic's own accounts at these providers:

  • Google (Sheets + Gmail) - hosts the CRM and sends email. Google Privacy
  • n8n - runs the automation workflows. n8n Privacy
  • Booking/contact tool - demo calls are handled through Calendly/Cal.com if connected, or through the email handoff on the booking page until a scheduler is live.

I do not share data with advertisers and do not sell data.

6. How long I keep data

  • Prospect data: up to 24 months from last contact unless you ask me to delete it sooner.
  • Clinic data: for the lifetime of the engagement plus 12 months for tax and accounting records.
  • End-client data: managed by the clinic inside the clinic's Sheet, retained according to the clinic's policy. The clinic can delete records at any time.

7. Your rights

You can:

  • Ask what data I hold about you.
  • Ask me to correct or delete it.
  • Ask me to stop emailing you (every email includes an opt-out).
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (GDPR/UK GDPR/CPRA territories).

To exercise these rights, email vibekb.1234@gmail.com. I respond within 30 days.

8. Security

  • All webhook traffic between dashboard, n8n, and Apps Script is authenticated via an X-Webhook-Secret header.
  • Dashboard passcodes are SHA-256 hashed in the browser before being saved to localStorage. Plain-text passcodes are never persisted.
  • The Google API key used by the dashboard is restricted to the Sheets API and HTTP-referrer-restricted to the dashboard domain.
  • OAuth credentials live in the clinic's n8n instance and are managed by the clinic's Google Workspace admin.

No system is fully breach-proof. If a breach affects you, I will notify you and (where required) the relevant authority within 72 hours of discovery.

9. International transfers

Google and n8n may process data outside your country (typically US or EU). I rely on Standard Contractual Clauses and provider DPAs for these transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

If I make a material change, I will update the "Last updated" date at the top and email active clinic clients. Continued use after the change means you accept the updated terms.
