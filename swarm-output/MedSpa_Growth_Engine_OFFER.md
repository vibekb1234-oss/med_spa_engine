Meanwhile you have 400 past clients in a spreadsheet who haven't heard from you since they checked out.

## What's Inside (Deliverables)

- **Workflow 1 — Lead Inquiry Capture:** Webhook-triggered instant response to new inquiries from any source (website form, landing page, intake link) — sends a personalized reply within minutes with service info, booking link, and a soft qualifier
- **Workflow 2 — Booking Confirmation:** Triggered on every new appointment — sends a branded confirmation email with treatment prep instructions, what to bring, and parking/arrival details (reduces day-of cancellations by removing uncertainty)
- **Workflow 3 — 48-Hour Appointment Reminder:** Automated reminder two days before each appointment — conversational tone, includes a one-click confirm or reschedule link so clients self-manage rather than ghosting
- **Workflow 4 — 24-Hour Final Confirmation:** Day-before reminder with treatment-specific prep notes (e.g., arrive with clean skin, no retinol for 3 days) — the message that converts the remaining hesitant clients
- **Workflow 5 — No-Show Recovery:** Triggers within 2 hours of a missed appointment — empathetic, non-accusatory re-booking offer with a soft urgency element (limited availability window) — recovers 20–30% of no-shows before the slot is lost for good
- **Workflow 6 — Post-Treatment Review and Referral Ask:** Sends 48 hours after treatment — first asks for a Google review (captures satisfaction at peak happiness), then at Day 7 sends a referral ask with a simple "send this link to a friend" mechanic
- **Workflow 7 — VIP Retention Sequence:** Monthly cron identifies clients who have spent above a threshold or visited 3+ times — sends VIP-tier outreach (early access to new treatments, loyalty offers, personal check-in) to increase 6-month return rate
- **Workflow 8 — Weekly Performance Report:** Every Monday at 8 AM you receive a full pipeline summary: new inquiries, confirmed appointments, no-show rate, reviews generated, referrals sent, revenue-at-risk flags for upcoming gaps in the schedule
- **Google Sheets CRM:** Pre-configured 3-tab tracker (Clients, Referral Partners, Activity Log) — your single source of truth, synced with every automation
- **Config Wizard:** One-page HTML tool to configure all 8 workflows in a single 10-minute session
- **Full Handoff Guide:** Step-by-step usage guide written for your front desk team, not for engineers
