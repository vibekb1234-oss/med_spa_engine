## How To Sell It

Primary positioning:
"A done-for-you MedSpa Growth OS that automates client follow-up, no-show recovery, reviews, referrals, and lapsed-client reactivation around your existing booking system."

Do not call it SaaS.

Call it:
- Managed automation system.
- Done-for-you Growth OS.
- Client retention engine.
- Follow-up and review automation layer.

## How To Deliver It

Use this order:

1. Discovery call.
2. ROI calculator.
3. Offer tier selection.
4. Client onboarding form.
5. Setup guide.
6. Pre-launch checklist.
7. Demo data smoke test.
8. Live batch launch.
9. Seven-day monitoring.
10. First weekly report.
11. Monthly campaign and optimization cadence.

## How To Keep It Low-Complexity

Do:
- Use Gmail, Google Sheets, n8n, and static HTML.
- Customize copy through templates.
- Keep sensitive clinical records out of the system.
- Charge extra for custom integrations.
- Use the same dashboard and workflow structure for every clinic.

Do not:
- Build a multi-tenant app yet.
- Add user billing or self-serve signup.
- Promise direct booking-system sync unless scoped.
- Add SMS until email version has proven demand.
- Store medical records or treatment photos.
