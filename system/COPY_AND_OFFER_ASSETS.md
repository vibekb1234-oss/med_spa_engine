## Positioning Rule

Lead with:

**Lost Revenue Recovery OS for MedSpas**

Do not lead with:

- AI automation
- SaaS
- CRM software
- Generic medspa marketing
- Chatbot

The offer is a managed recovery layer installed around the clinic's existing booking process.

## Copy Guardrails

Use:

- "modeled upside"
- "designed to recover"
- "helps reduce leakage"
- "installed around your existing booking process"
- "owner visibility"
- "recovery workflows"

Avoid:

- guaranteed revenue
- guaranteed bookings
- guaranteed reviews
- fake testimonials
- claims that the dashboard is server-side secure without protected hosting
- claims that the system replaces a booking platform, EHR, EMR, or compliance process

## Best Current Pitch

We install a Lost Revenue Recovery OS for independent MedSpas so missed consults, no-shows, cold leads, lapsed VIP clients, reviews, referrals, and owner reporting are handled consistently without replacing the clinic's booking system.

This is where the sales and messaging assets live. Keep offer claims conservative and modeled, not guaranteed.

## Messaging Rules

- Position as a niche managed operating system for medspas, not generic SaaS.
- Talk about missed revenue, response speed, no-shows, review generation, referrals, and lapsed-client recovery.
- Do not guarantee revenue outcomes.
- Use conservative phrases: "modeled upside", "designed to recover", "helps reduce", "tracks", "surfaces", "automates".
- Keep the offer simple: setup fee plus monthly retainer.
