# MedSpa Growth Engine — Post-Discovery Call Follow-Up
## 3-Email Sequence | After a positive discovery call

**When to use:** Prospect was engaged, asked good questions, said "let me think about it" or "I'll get back to you" at the end of the call. Send One_Pager.html immediately after the call, then start this sequence.

---

> **Context note:** At this stage the prospect has seen the demo, understands the system, and is interested. The conversion blockers are usually: (1) "is this worth the setup fee?", (2) "will I actually use it?", (3) procrastination / being busy. These emails address each one in order.

## SENDING NOTES

**Timing:**
- Immediately after call: send One_Pager.html + Email 1
- Day 3: Email 2 (if no reply — if they replied positively, move to closing)
- Day 7: Email 3 (final follow-up)
- Day 30+: Add back to cold email sequence if still no response

**What to do if they reply "not now":**
Reply: "Totally understand — when would be a better time to revisit? I'll put a reminder in my calendar and reach back out then. Good luck with [something specific they mentioned]."

**What to do if they ask for a discount:**
Hold the setup fee if possible (it's a quality signal). Offer to reduce the first month's retainer by $200 as a "pilot month" incentive. Don't discount both.

**What to do if they ask for references:**
"I protect client privacy, but I'm happy to share a before/after case study in full — I can walk you through the exact numbers on a quick 10-minute call."

**Close rate benchmark:**
- If they agreed to the call AND attended → close rate should be 40–60%
- Most deals close by Email 2 if they're going to close at all
- Email 3 is a long-term seed — some reply 2–4 weeks later
