# MedSpa Growth Engine — Discovery Call Script
## The 20-Minute Call That Closes $3,500–$5,000 Deals

---

> **Before the call:**
> - Know their clinic name, location, services offered
> - Check their Google reviews count and rating
> - Check their Instagram — note follower count, post frequency, whether they post testimonials
> - Check their website for a booking system (Fresha, Jane App, Mindbody, Square = they're organised)
> - Have the demo (or Loom video) ready to share screen

---

## OPENING (2 min)

*"Hey [NAME], great to finally connect. I've blocked 20 minutes — does that still work?"*

[Wait for yes]

*"Perfect. Quick agenda: I'll ask you a few questions about where [CLINIC NAME] may be leaking revenue — missed consults, no-shows, cold leads, lapsed VIPs, and reviews. Then I'll show you exactly what I've built. At the end, you tell me honestly if it's a fit. Sound good?"*

---

## DISCOVERY (8 min)

**0. The recovery frame:**
*"Before we talk tools, I want to find the lost-revenue moments. Usually they sit in four places: missed consults, no-shows, cold leads who never booked, and VIP clients who have gone quiet. Which one feels most real for you right now?"*

**1. The operation:**
*"How many treatments are you doing per week on average right now?"*

*"Is it mostly injectables, or a mix of services?"*

[20–50 treatments/week = perfect. Under 15 = small fit, may need to adjust. Over 80 = they have a team, system is even more valuable.]

**2. The no-show question — this is your opener:**
*"What does your no-show rate look like? Like, out of every 10 appointments, how many actually show up?"*

[Industry average is 2–3 no-shows per 10. If they say 1–2, great — the system prevents that from getting worse. If they say 3–5, this is a huge pain.]

*"And when someone no-shows — what happens? Does anyone reach out to them?"*

[Listen for: nobody chases, they might call once, they manually send a text — but it's inconsistent and time-consuming]

**3. The follow-up question:**
*"After a client comes in for a treatment — do you have a system for following up with them afterward? Like, checking in, asking for a review, or reminding them it's time to come back?"*

[Almost nobody does this systematically. Let them describe their current process — or lack of one.]

**4. The retention number — your emotional anchor:**
*"Ballpark — out of your new clients who come in for a first treatment, what percentage do you think come back for a second visit?"*

[They'll either say they don't know (which is revealing) or guess 40–60%. Industry average is 30–40% without a retention system. This number will shock them if you know it.]

*"The industry average without a follow-up system is about 30–40%. Meaning 6 out of 10 new clients you worked hard to acquire never come back. That's the gap this system closes."*

**5. The Google reviews question:**
*"How many Google reviews does [CLINIC NAME] have? And what's your rating?"*

[If under 100 reviews: "That's the most common gap I see — clinics with great treatments that just don't have a system to collect reviews consistently."]

**6. The time question:**
*"Roughly how much time are you or your team spending per week on client follow-up, reminders, and outreach? If you had to guess."*

[Listen for: "basically none" (the problem is invisible to them) or "way too much" (the pain is obvious). Either way it's a problem.]

**7. The dream question:**
*"If the follow-up, the no-show prevention, the review collection — if all of that ran on autopilot, what would you do with that time and energy?"*

[Their answer = dream outcome. Use their exact words when you close.]

## BRIDGE (1 min)

*"This is really helpful. So what I'm hearing is: [2–3 lost-revenue moments in their words]. That's exactly what the MedSpa Growth Engine was built to recover. Let me show you."*

---

## DEMO (6 min)

**1. New inquiry — instant personalised response (1 min)**
*"So someone fills out your contact form — let's say they're asking about Botox. Here's what they get within seconds:"*
→ Show the service info email — professional, branded, personalised to Botox
*"They didn't have to wait for you to reply. They got all the info they needed, with a clear CTA to book. And you got an instant alert with their details."*

**2. The appointment follow-up chain (1.5 min)**
*"Now they've booked. 48 hours before the appointment:"*
→ Show the 48hr reminder email
*"24 hours before:"*
→ Show the 24hr final reminder
*"If they no-show — this goes out the same afternoon:"*
→ Show the no-show recovery email — warm, not pushy, with a re-book button
*"The reminders and recovery email give clients a clean chance to confirm, reschedule, or come back before that revenue disappears. We will model the impact from your real appointment volume instead of guessing."*

**3. The review request — the compounding asset (1 min)**
*"The appointment is complete. 24 hours later, this goes to the client:"*
→ Show the review request email — beautiful, one-click link to Google review page
*"If they're a returning client — visit number 2 or more — they can also get this, depending on the referral rules you approve:"*
→ Show the referral email with the 15% friend discount + $50 credit
*"The point is consistency. You ask after every completed visit without relying on the front desk to remember. Even a modest response rate compounds over time."*

**4. The retention sequences (1 min)**
*"Now for the clients who haven't been back in a while:"*
→ Show the 60-day rebook nudge
→ Show the 90-day "we miss you" with 10% discount
→ Show the seasonal promo email concept
*"Every month on the 1st, every active client gets a seasonal offer — spring skin refresh, summer body treatments, whatever's relevant. Automatic. You never have to plan or write a promotional email."*

**5. The dashboard (30 sec)**
*"And you see everything in one place — the revenue recovery pulse, client status, total visits, last contact, revenue per client, reviews requested vs received."*

*If the system prevents only a few missed appointments per month, the numbers can start to make sense before we even count reviews, referrals, or lapsed-client reactivation. We'll run that model from your actual numbers before you decide.*"

*I've got [X] setup slots this month. If you want to move forward this week, we can have it live within 7 days."*

**The ask:**
*"Do you want to move forward?"*

[Stop talking. Wait.]

## OBJECTION HANDLING

**"I already use [Mindbody / Jane App / Fresha] for bookings."**
> "This works alongside your booking system, not instead of it. Those platforms handle the calendar — this handles everything that happens around the appointment: the reminders, the follow-ups, the review requests, the re-booking campaigns. They don't do that automatically the way this system does."

**"My receptionist/front desk handles this."**
> "What does she do when she's not chasing no-shows and sending reminders? Most front desk staff say they're spending 2–3 hours a day on follow-up work that could be automated. This frees her up for work that actually requires a human — booking calls, handling questions, upselling at the desk."

**"I'm already pretty busy — I don't want more clients right now."**
> "This system isn't just about getting more clients — it's about retaining the ones you've already paid to acquire. Your highest-profit client is one who came 3–4 times. The system is designed to get every first-time client to a second and third visit. That's pure revenue growth without any new client acquisition cost."

**"Can I just do the review request part?"**
> "I don't offer individual pieces — the system works as a whole because the parts reinforce each other. A client who gets a check-in email is more likely to leave a review. A client who gets a re-booking sequence is more likely to refer a friend. You lose the compounding effect if you only do one piece."

**"$3,500 seems like a lot."**
> "Let me put it in context. If we use your actual monthly appointments, average treatment value, and no-show estimate, we can see how many appointments need to be protected for the system to make sense. If the math only works with aggressive assumptions, I would rather tell you that before you buy."

**"I need to think about it / talk to my partner."**
> "Completely fair. What's the main hesitation — is it the price, the timing, or do you want to see more detail first?" [Address it.] "I can hold a spot for 48 hours while you chat with your partner. After that I'll have to open it up. Would that work?"
