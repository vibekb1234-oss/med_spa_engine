## SENDING NOTES

**Lead sources for MedSpas:**
- Google Maps: "medspa [city]", "aesthetic clinic [city]", "botox clinic [city]"
- Instagram: search location tags for your city + #medspa #aesthetics — find clinic owner accounts
- RealSelf.com: provider directory filtered by location
- LocalMed / Zocdoc provider listings
- Yelp: "medical spa [city]"

**Ideal targets:**
- 1–3 providers (owner often is the primary injector)
- Google reviews: 30–150 (growing, but not yet dominant — they need more)
- Instagram: active but not large following (500–5,000 followers)
- No obvious automation in their Instagram (no drip campaigns, no re-booking sequences mentioned)
- In business 2–5 years (established client base, hungry to grow)

**Red flags — avoid:**
- Part of a chain or franchise (no autonomy, decisions made elsewhere)
- Medical practices with MedSpa add-on (dermatology, plastic surgery — different decision process)
- Just opened (no existing client base to retain)

**Personalisation for best results:**
- Mention their Google review count: "I noticed [CLINIC NAME] has 47 Google reviews — this system would help you ask consistently after every completed visit"
- Mention a specific service: "I saw you specialise in filler and Botox — I built the system specifically with injection treatment cycles in mind"
- Instagram observation: "Your Instagram posts look great — the system I built feeds that momentum with a backend client retention engine"
