# MedSpa Growth Engine - Offer Tiers

Use these tiers to sell the same core system with different levels of service. Do not build custom infrastructure for each tier unless the client pays for custom scope.

## Positioning Rule

Lead with the flagship mechanism:

**Lost Revenue Recovery OS for MedSpas**

The tiers should feel like stronger versions of the same recovery system, not random automation bundles. Reviews, referrals, reminders, reporting, and VIP retention are all framed as ways to recover money already leaking from the clinic.

## Front-End Offers

Use these to create demand before pitching the full install.

### Free - Revenue Leak Snapshot

Best for outbound, Instagram, and landing-page opt-ins.

The prospect sends appointment volume, average treatment value, no-show rate, review count, and lapsed-client count. You return a simple modeled leak map showing the biggest recovery opportunities.

CTA:
"Map my clinic's leaks."

### Paid - Recovery Opportunity Audit

Best for warmer prospects who want a real diagnosis before buying.

**Price:** $297-$750, credited toward setup if they buy within 14 days.

Included:
- Follow-up gap review.
- No-show and lapsed-client model.
- Review/referral consistency review.
- Recommended install plan.
- 20-minute walkthrough.

Best close line:
"This shows exactly where the clinic is leaking follow-up before we install anything."

## Tier 1 - Recovery Core

Best for owner-injectors who need the basics fixed fast.

**Setup:** $3,500  
**Monthly:** $1,000/month

Included:
- Lead inquiry intake and instant email response.
- Booking confirmation from dashboard.
- 48hr and 24hr appointment reminders.
- No-show recovery email.
- Post-treatment check-in.
- Google review request.
- Weekly performance report.
- Google Sheets CRM and clinic dashboard.
- 30-day launch support.

Not included:
- Seasonal promo calendar.
- Monthly campaign writing.
- SMS.
- Booking-system API integration.
- Multi-location reporting.

Best close line:
"This fixes the daily leakage: missed consults, reminders, no-shows, reviews, and owner visibility."

## Tier 2 - Revenue Recovery Engine

Best default offer. This should be the main pitch.

**Setup:** $5,000  
**Monthly:** $1,500/month

Everything in Recovery Core, plus:
- VIP and lapsed-client reactivation sequences.
- Referral ask for returning clients.
- Monthly seasonal campaign loaded from the campaign calendar.
- Monthly ROI review email.
- One monthly copy/timing optimization.
- Quarterly treatment-menu refresh.

Best close line:
"This turns your existing leads, no-shows, and VIP client base into a recovery engine."

## Tier 3 - Local Dominance

Best for higher-volume clinics with existing staff.

**Setup:** $7,500+  
**Monthly:** $2,500/month

Everything in Revenue Recovery Engine, plus:
- Two campaign angles per month.
- Quarterly Google review growth plan.
- Staff workflow audit.
- Custom dashboard views for provider, service, or lead source.
- Monthly 30-minute performance call.
- Light lead-source attribution cleanup.

Bill separately:
- SMS.
- Paid ads.
- Website rebuild.
- Booking-system API work.
- Multi-location reporting.
- HIPAA/legal review.

Best close line:
"This gives you the follow-up discipline of a larger clinic without adding another full-time operator."

## Recommended Sales Path

Lead with Growth Engine.
Say "Revenue Recovery Engine" on calls when the clinic is high-value or retention-heavy.

If they are price-sensitive:
- Drop to Retention Core.
- Keep the same setup quality.
- Remove monthly campaigns and VIP/lapsed-client optimization.

If they are growth-hungry:
- Upgrade to Local Dominance.
- Keep the technical backbone the same.
- Increase strategy, campaign, reporting, and review cadence.

## Scope Control Language

"The system works alongside your booking platform. It does not replace your booking platform or medical record system. Custom integrations, SMS, paid ads, website edits, and new workflow builds are scoped separately."

## Risk Reversal

Use an install guarantee, not a revenue guarantee.

"If we do not install the agreed recovery workflows, dashboard, intake tracking, and weekly report within 14 business days after receiving the required access and clinic information, we keep working at no extra charge until the agreed install is complete."

Optional stronger version for serious buyers:

"If we miss the agreed install window because of our side, your first month of support is free."
