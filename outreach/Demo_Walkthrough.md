# MedSpa Growth Engine - 12 Minute Demo Walkthrough

Use this to sell the system without overexplaining the tech.

## Before The Demo

Have these open:
- `dashboard/index.html` with demo data.
- `dashboard/Email_Preview.html`.
- `outreach/ROI_Calculator.html`.
- `setup/Pre_Launch_Checklist.md`.

Collect or estimate:
- Monthly appointments.
- Average treatment value.
- Current no-show estimate.
- Google review count.
- Main service line.

## Demo Flow

### 1. Frame The Problem - 90 Seconds

"Most clinics do not have one huge growth problem. They have revenue leaking from four places: missed consults, no-shows, cold leads, and lapsed VIP clients."

"This system is the recovery layer around your existing booking system. It closes those leaks without asking your team to live in another app."

### 2. Show The Dashboard - 2 Minutes

Point out:
- Revenue recovery pulse.
- New inquiries.
- Upcoming appointments.
- No-shows.
- VIP/lapsed clients.
- Weekly report metrics.

Say:
"This is not the medical record. It is the growth and follow-up command center."

### 3. Show The Daily Actions - 2 Minutes

Show:
- Confirm booking.
- Mark appointment complete.
- Status update.

Say:
"The clinic team mainly uses this for exceptions and confirmations. The repetitive follow-up happens in the background."

### 4. Show The Email Previews - 2 Minutes

Show:
- New inquiry service info.
- 48hr reminder.
- 24hr reminder.
- Review request.
- Lapsed-client win-back.

Say:
"Every clinic gets copy tuned to their services and tone, but the system structure stays the same. That is how we keep delivery fast."

### 5. Run The ROI Calculator - 3 Minutes

Use their numbers.

Default conservative inputs:
- 120 appointments/month.
- $300 average treatment value.
- 8% missed appointment estimate.
- 25% recovery rate on missed appointments.
- 2 lapsed clients reactivated/month.
- 1 referred client/month.

Say:
"This is not a guarantee. It is a decision model. If the recovery math does not make sense conservatively, we should not do the project."

### 6. Close The Next Step - 90 Seconds

Say:
"The next step is not a giant onboarding process. We collect your clinic details, review the copy rules, connect Gmail/Sheets on a screen share, run test data, then monitor the first week."

Ask:
"Do you want me to send the scope for the Core version or the Growth Engine version?"

## Objection Notes

**We already use Jane/Fresha/Mindbody.**  
"Perfect. Keep it. This handles the follow-up layer around your booking system."

**Is this SaaS?**  
"No. It is a managed automation system. You are not buying a generic login. We set it up, monitor it, and adjust it for your clinic."

**Is this HIPAA compliant?**  
"We design it to avoid sensitive medical data. We keep clinical notes and medical records inside your existing systems. If you need formal compliance review, we scope that separately."

**Can we just do reviews?**  
"Technically yes, but the value is stronger when reviews connect to the post-treatment and retention flow."
