# MedSpa Growth Engine
### The Lost Revenue Recovery OS for Independent Aesthetic Clinics

---

## The Problem

Most MedSpas are great at treatments. They're terrible at what happens around treatments.

- **Missed consults** go cold before anyone follows up properly
- **Missed appointments** quietly remove revenue that was already on the calendar
- **First-time clients** are easy to lose after visit one when there is no follow-up path
- **Google reviews** trickle in randomly instead of systematically — costing new clients who go to the clinic with more reviews
- **Seasonal promotions** never go out because the owner is too busy treating clients to write them

None of this is a talent problem. It's a systems problem.

---

## The Solution

**MedSpa Growth Engine** is a done-for-you Lost Revenue Recovery OS that installs the follow-up and visibility layer around your existing booking process, so missed consults, no-shows, cold leads, lapsed VIP clients, reviews, referrals, and owner reporting are handled consistently.

---

## What's Included

### 1 — Lead Inquiry Intake
When someone fills out your contact form:
- Instantly sends them personalised service information (6 service types: Botox, Filler, Laser Hair Removal, HydraFacial, Microneedling, and general)
- Alerts you with their details in seconds
- Adds them to your CRM automatically
- Follows up in 3 days if they haven't booked

Every inquiry gets a response in seconds. Nobody slips through the cracks.

### 2 — Appointment Follow-Up Engine
Runs every morning:
- **48-hour reminder** — gives clients enough time to confirm or reschedule
- **24-hour final reminder** — *"See you tomorrow at 2:30!"*
- **Same-day no-show recovery** — warm re-booking email sent to every no-show
- **Next-day post-treatment check-in** — aftercare tips + *"Ready to book your next visit?"*

No-shows are reduced. The ones who still miss get chased back automatically.

### 3 — Review & Referral System
- **24 hours after every completed appointment:** Beautiful, one-click Google review request
- **For returning clients (2+ visits):** Referral ask with a 15% discount for their friend and a $50 credit for them

If you do 30 treatments a week, even a modest review response rate compounds because the request happens every time.

### 4 — Client Reactivation Engine
Runs monthly. Automatically sends:
- **60 days after last visit:** Gentle rebook nudge
- **90 days:** *"We miss you"* with 10% loyalty discount
- **180 days:** Personal win-back message
- **Monthly seasonal promo:** Spring refresh, summer body, fall renewal, New Year new you — relevant to the current month, every month, automatically

### 5 — Weekly Recovery Report
Every Monday morning, you receive a clean performance email with:
- Revenue from the past 7 days (with week-over-week trend)
- No-shows by name and recovery status
- At-risk clients (60–90 days since last visit)
- New clients added, VIP count, pending inquiries

You see the recovery activity of the system in your inbox every single week — without logging into anything.
