# MedSpa Growth Engine - Treatment Copy Library

Use this to make each deployment feel custom without rewriting the system. Keep medical claims conservative and have the clinic approve final copy.

## Copy Rules

- Use plain service categories, not private medical details.
- Do not promise medical outcomes.
- Keep pricing as "starting at" or "package options available" unless the clinic confirms exact pricing.
- Keep aftercare and prep instructions general unless the clinic approves specific instructions.
- Add opt-out language to promotional or reactivation emails when appropriate.

## Botox / Dysport

**Positioning:** Fast, subtle wrinkle-softening with a natural look.

Inquiry response block:
"Thanks for reaching out about Botox/Dysport. Most clients choose this treatment to soften expression lines while keeping a natural look. A consultation helps confirm the right areas, dosing range, and timing for your goals."

Prep note:
"Before your visit, avoid alcohol and blood-thinning supplements if your provider has advised you to do so. Arrive with clean skin when possible."

Rebooking timing:
"Many clients plan maintenance every 3-4 months, depending on their goals and provider recommendation."

Review ask angle:
"If your visit felt smooth and well cared for, your review helps other clients feel confident booking their first appointment."

Win-back angle:
"It may be time to plan your next maintenance visit if you want to stay ahead of expression lines."
