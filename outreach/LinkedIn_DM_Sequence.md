## FINDING MEDSPA OWNERS ON LINKEDIN

MedSpa owners are less active on LinkedIn than CPAs or mortgage brokers — use Instagram + Google Maps as primary channels, LinkedIn as secondary.

**LinkedIn Search:**
- Title: "Owner" OR "Founder" OR "Medical Director" OR "Clinic Owner"
- Company: search for company name after finding on Google Maps
- Industry: Health, Wellness and Fitness

**Better approach — Instagram DMs (higher ROI for MedSpas):**
Search Instagram for: #medspa[yourcity] #aestheticclinic #botoxclinic #medspaforyou
→ Find clinic owner accounts (look for the one posting from the owner's perspective)
→ Engage with 2–3 posts before DMing (like, leave a genuine comment)
→ Send Instagram DM using the same sequence above

**Instagram DM Note (tweak the tone — more casual):**
> "Hey [NAME]! Love what you're building at [CLINIC]. I help MedSpas automate their client follow-up, no-show recovery, and Google reviews — the stuff that's easy to let slip when you're busy treating clients. Would you be open to a quick chat? 🙏"
